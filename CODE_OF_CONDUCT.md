# Code of Conduct

This is a personal open-source project. Everyone participating in its issues, pull requests and discussions should be able to contribute without harassment.

Be respectful and specific. Discuss the work and its effects, explain disagreements, welcome questions and credit contributions. Respect privacy, accessibility needs and different backgrounds or levels of experience.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexualized comments, impersonation, deliberate disruption and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not use project spaces for spam, unwanted solicitation or attempts to obtain credentials.

Report a concern privately to WizardLoop through [Telegram](https://t.me/wizardloop) or the published email, [wizard@wizardloop.com](mailto:wizard@wizardloop.com). Share only what is needed to understand the incident. Do not publish private evidence in an issue or circulate it unnecessarily.

The maintainer may request changes, remove inappropriate material or restrict participation to protect the project. The response should consider the conduct, its impact and whether it continued after a request to stop. No fixed response time or confidential reporting platform is promised by this document.

These expectations apply to this repository and project spaces managed by its maintainer, and when someone explicitly represents the project elsewhere. This document does not claim authority over unrelated communities.
