# Code of Conduct

This is my personal open-source project. I want everyone participating in its issues, pull requests and discussions to be able to contribute without harassment.

Be respectful and specific. Discuss the work and its effects, explain disagreements, welcome questions and credit contributions. Respect privacy, accessibility needs and different backgrounds or levels of experience.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexualized comments, impersonation, deliberate disruption and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not use project spaces for spam, unwanted solicitation or attempts to obtain credentials.

Report a concern to me privately through [Telegram](https://t.me/wizardloop) or [wizard@wizardloop.com](mailto:wizard@wizardloop.com). Share only what is needed to understand the incident. Do not publish private evidence in an issue or circulate it unnecessarily.

I may request changes, remove inappropriate material or restrict participation to protect the project. I consider the conduct, its impact and whether it continued after a request to stop. I do not promise a fixed response time or a dedicated confidential reporting platform.

These expectations apply to this repository and project spaces I manage, and when someone explicitly represents the project elsewhere. I do not claim authority over unrelated communities.
